AMOS

Automatic Medicine Ordering System

AMOS is an AI-powered platform designed to help patients manage long-term medications. It predicts medicine depletion, sends reminders, and automatically reorders medicines from nearby pharmacies to ensure uninterrupted treatment.

Key Features

Everything needed to reduce missed doses and avoid running out of important medicines.

Smart Depletion Prediction

Estimates when medicines run low based on dosage, stock, and refill level.

Automatic Reordering

Creates timely refill actions before stock reaches critical levels.

Nearby Pharmacy Discovery

Helps identify convenient pharmacies for quicker medicine access.

Dose Reminders

Organizes daily reminders by date and time to improve adherence.

Prescription Upload

Stores prescriptions in one place for quick verification and future orders.

Simple Dashboard

View medicine status, reminders, and activity from a clean interface.
